# Implementation walkthrough

1. Identify the qualifying decision and proposed effect.
2. Declare authority, mandate, scope, and affected operational communities.
3. Identify situated-knowledge dependencies and bearer classes.
4. Map channels, including an unanticipated-input route.
5. Retrieve comparable incidents and assess relevance.
6. Produce an independent epistemic impact assessment.
7. Open the configured objection window.
8. Admit, suspend, modify, withdraw, or override the effect.
9. Produce decision, challenge, override, and effect-status receipts.
10. Expire the assessment when time or operating conditions invalidate it.
