# Epistemic impact assessment

The assessment binds a proposed decision and effect to declared knowledge dependencies, bearer classes, consultation channels, prior incidents, contested findings, residual risks, assessor authority, validity, and appeal.

Outcomes are `adequate`, `inadequate`, or `indeterminate`. `Adequate` is bounded by scope and time. Material operational change expires the result and requires reassessment.
